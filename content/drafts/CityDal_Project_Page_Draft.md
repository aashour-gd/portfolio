### What it is
CityDal is a city-discovery product for Qatar. In the user app, people find nearby places, events and deals, save favourites, book a time, chat with a business and earn points by inviting friends. In CityDal Business, owners set up a business, publish services and deals, manage bookings and see how many people viewed and saved them. The product collected its places, stores and events from Google. [CONFIRM wording for the data source.]
