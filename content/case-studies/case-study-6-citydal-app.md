# CityDal: one city guide, two apps

![CityDal brand guidelines cover: the CityDal wordmark and the tagline "Reveal the City's Treasured Mysteries" beside an oversized version of the logo mark.](/assets/citydal/brand_cover.png)

## What it is

CityDal is a city-discovery product for Qatar. In the user app, people find nearby places, events and deals, save favourites, book a time, chat with a business and earn points by inviting friends. In CityDal Business, owners set up a business, publish services and deals, manage bookings and see how many people viewed and saved them.

## My role

I owned the design end to end in 2023: the brand identity, the user app, and the provider app, CityDal Business.

## The brand

The identity is built to be one thing across both apps and to survive the smallest place a brand lives — an app icon on a home screen. The mark fuses a C, a d and a map pin into a single shape, so it reads as a letter and as "a place" at once.

![The CityDal horizontal logo: the logomark paired with the "CityDal" wordmark and the tagline.](/assets/citydal/citydal-11.png)

The palette is ten colours — two primaries (Coral Red and Cosmic Cobalt), four secondary colours, and four neutrals — kept deliberately tight so the two primaries stay the brand. Inter carries the type in three weights.

![The CityDal colour palette: two primary brand colours, four secondary colours and four neutrals, with hex values.](/assets/citydal/citydal-26.png)

![CityDal uses Inter in Regular, SemiBold and Bold.](/assets/citydal/citydal-30.png)

## Two apps, one mark

CityDal and CityDal Business share the same mark and wordmark; the provider app adds a small "Business" label, so the logo changes with the app a person opens.

## What shipped, and what happened

After about six months, the client discontinued the product. I'm showing the design work and what I learned, not business results I can't verify.
